Once upon a time, in a small village, lived a curious little girl named Mina. She loved to explore the forest near her home and make friends with the animals.

One day Mina found a tiny bird with a hurt wing. She gently carried it home and cared for it. Mina's kindness drew the attention of her neighbor, an old woodcutter, who had never been kind to animals.

The bird's wing healed, and Mina released it back into the sky. Seeing this, the woodcutter realized how small acts of care could make a difference.

From then on, he started leaving food for the forest animals and helped Mina plant new trees. The village grew greener and happier, and everyone learned that kindness spreads.

Moral: Small acts of kindness can change a community.
